ARTI 303 - Lab 04: Variables in Prolog
Task 2 - Family queries and answers
Based on the family facts on page 6 of Lab 04.

1. Show the names of all females.
   ?- female(Name).
   Name = sara ;
   Name = nora.

2. Show whether there is any male.
   ?- male(_).
   true.

3. Show the grandparent of Faisal.
   ?- parent(Grandparent, Parent), parent(Parent, faisal).
   Grandparent = ali,
   Parent = muhammad.

4. Show all daughters of Ahmad.
   ?- parent(ahmad, Daughter), female(Daughter).
   Daughter = sara ;
   Daughter = nora.

5. Show whether Ahmad and Muhammad have a common parent.
   ?- parent(CommonParent, ahmad), parent(CommonParent, muhammad).
   CommonParent = ali.

6. Show whether Khan is the brother of Nora.
   ?- male(khan), parent(Parent, khan), parent(Parent, nora), khan \= nora.
   Parent = ahmad.
   The query succeeds, so Khan is Nora's brother.
